NextHand prototype (Flipkart APM '29, Prompt 2). Pulkit Maheshwari, BITS Pilani.

HOW TO RUN
Unzip, then double-click index.html (Chrome, Edge or Firefox). No install and no internet needed.
Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Nothing is sent anywhere; state is kept in your browser.
Works on a laptop and on a phone (on a phone the left rail becomes the header, and "Reset demo" sits under "Demo controls").
"Reset demo" (bottom of the left rail) puts everything back to the start.

WHAT THIS IS
The NextHand product as responders see it. Callers never use NextHand: they only use their own phone.
Calls, the five keypad questions, call-backs and confirmation calls are automated and happen outside
the product; NextHand only shows what they produced. Everything uses 2018 technology: keypad menus, SMS,
recorded scripts and rules. No AI.

EVERY CASE IS BUILT FROM WHAT THE CALLER GAVE
Five keypad presses, most important first: 1 problem, 2 is life at risk, 3 when it gets worse, 4 how many
people, 5 who is calling. Plus where the call came from (the phone network, or a PIN and landmark when the
caller reports for someone else) and a recorded voice message with a name and landmark.
The case title, the band and the "why it is ranked" sentence are all worked out from those presses:
  Immediate  life at risk, or worse within the hour with fire, gas, flood or collapse
  Urgent     hurt but awake, or worse today
  Delayed    the rest
A "not sure" press sends the call to the review desk. Open any case to see exactly what was pressed.

THREE PEOPLE CAN USE IT (buttons at the top of the left rail)
  Duty officer   decides who goes. Sees ranked cases, accepts or declines, reviews requests from
                 partner NGOs, checks unclear calls, sees every unit's status.
  Verified NGO   a trusted partner. Takes Local-tier cases, and can ask to take higher-severity cases.
  New NGO        on probation. Sees Delayed cases only; the exact place, number and name unlock on accept.

TWO-MINUTE TOUR (also shown at the top of the screen, ticked off as you go)
1. Press "Simulate a new call" (left rail): a pumping-station case arrives, ranked Urgent from its keypad answers.
2. Switch to the verified NGO > Higher severity > "Request this case". Type why your team is right for it.
3. Switch to the duty officer > Requests. The checks have already run. Approve it, or decline with a note.
4. Switch back to the NGO. The case is in "Your tasks". Open it: "Call the caller" asks you to confirm what
   was pressed and the exact place, then record one outcome. Step it through to "Task done". The case now
   says "Awaiting confirmation". Use the demo buttons to play what the caller presses.
5. As the duty officer, decline the dam case. Pick "Other" and type your own reason. The case moves up
   a tier and leaves your list; the reason goes into its audit trail.
6. Open the new NGO view to see what probation hides. Finish 3 tasks and it graduates.

THE RESPONDER'S CALL-BACK (after Accept)
Four outcomes: Confirmed; Worse than reported (band goes up); Less serious (band goes down); Not genuine
(case closes and the caller's number gets a strike, shown on any later case from that number).
For Immediate cases the unit can move first and the call is made on the way.
Once the unit is "On the way", a map shows the unit, the route and the caller's pin.

ALSO TRY
- "Simulate a repeat call": first press, a second caller about the same place with the same answers is merged
  into the case. Second press, the same number calls again with worse answers and the band goes up.
- Accept the dam case, then "+3 hours": the caller misses two automated call-backs, so the unit goes anyway
  and asks locally using the name and landmark left in the voice message.
- Duty officer > Review desk: a call with "not sure" keypad answers. Raising it to Immediate counts as a
  miss in the audit figure.
- "+1 hour" / "+3 hours": offers that nobody accepts climb Local > District > State > National and
  leave the earlier tier's list.
- Units tab: send an SMS status check. A status older than 2 hours counts as unavailable.
- Network is down: offers go by SMS instead.
- Play the voice message on any case (demo text only: there is no real audio).

WHAT IS SIMULATED
The callers' keypad answers and voice messages, the experts, the units' SMS replies and the map (a drawing, not
real geography). Demo controls (left rail) are not part of the product. Offer windows (5 min / 15 min / 8 h), the
tasks needed to request higher-severity cases (3 Urgent, 6 Immediate), the 60 km limit, time limits and goals are
placeholders. The ranking inside a band uses people, severity and time; the screens never show the maths.
All people, places, phone numbers and figures are invented.
